## Day 1 — ALIGN: Mission, Goals, and How We Work with Customers

**Monday | Half-day (starts ~12:00) | Not everyone present**

**Theme**: Follow up on the mission & goals meeting. Gather feedback, debate, and declare "version one." Understand how we work with customers through Philippe Kocic's testimony.

> David, Constantin, and Natalia are out. Carlos joins from 2pm (remote). Tim is present. This is the leadership alignment day — the team catches up via recordings and generated summaries.

### Working Lunch Kickoff (12:00 – 13:00)
**Format**: Informal start over lunch

Paolo opens:
- Welcome, context for the week, the emotional contract: *"Deliver 10X by working 0.5X"*
- Brief recap: what happened at the One AEM workshop, why we're here, what the week looks like
- Set the tone: this transformation is about elevation, not extraction. If it feels like burnout, it's broken.

### Session 1 (13:00 – 14:15): Mission & Goals — Feedback and V1
**Format**: Presentation + open discussion

Follow-up to the Feb 2 team meeting:
1. **Recap the mission** (10 min): *"Accelerate AEM growth and adoption by deeply engaging with customers to discover, validate, and scale the accelerators that drive their success."*
2. **The accelerator model** (15 min): Experiment → Customer Use → Validated → Made to Product. The three impact phases: Acquisition, Adoption, Expansion. Experience Catalyst and Digital Insights as established initiatives.
3. **Open feedback round** (30 min): What resonates? What's unclear? What's missing? Refine the mission and goals live.
4. **Declare V1** (20 min): Not final, but a starting point. Flexible, but written down. The team has a shared reference from today.

**Key topics to surface**:
- Usage as the driving metric (not direct dollar revenue)
- How we balance independent thought + direct customer focus with staying connected to other AEM teams
- The 6+1 value framework: where does each accelerator create value?

### Session 2 (14:30 – 16:00): Guest — Philippe Kocic
**Format**: Testimony + Q&A

Philippe shares the Astra team's experience:
- The pancake chart methodology: hypothesis → experiment → customer engagement → product
- How a team of ~10 people runs parallel experiments with rapid iteration
- How they vibe code everything they do with customers
- Tracking metrics: customer interactions, experiments run, learnings converted to product
- Lessons learned: what worked, what didn't, what he'd do differently

**Connection exercise** (last 20 min): Map our accelerator pipeline onto Philippe's framework. Where are our experiments today? What would the pancake chart look like for us?

### Close (16:00 – 16:45): What We Heard + What's Ahead

## Day 2 — INSPIRE: What's Possible with AI

**Tuesday | Full day | Blended: presentations + hands-on**

**Theme**: Inspire the team with what's possible today — and what's coming. Not just for what we do now, but for what we'll have to do in the future. The world is changing; websites as we know them may not exist. Get hands dirty in the afternoon.

### Morning Spark (9:00 – 9:15)
**"What changed is the speed"** (Karl)
Cloud Co-Work: idea before Christmas, shipped in 10 days, written entirely by Claude Code. Experience Catalyst pivot: first prototype in 3-4 days, customers using it a week later. That's the new baseline.

### Session 1 (9:15 – 10:45): Guest — Lars Dierolf
**Format**: Talk + Q&A

Lars shares insights on AI-native engineering practice:
- How his team works day-to-day with AI tools
- What changed in planning, review, and shipping
- Practical patterns that moved them from "using AI" to "working through AI"
- Where the biggest gains came from — and what surprised them

**Hands-on tie-in**: Lars shares one concrete workflow the team can try in the afternoon.

### Session 2 (11:00 – 12:30): What's Beyond Vibe Coding
**Format**: Workshop + discussion

Vibe coding is the starting point, not the destination. This session covers the full operational picture:
- **The AI maturity framework**: Level 1 (AI User) → Level 2 (AI First) → Level 3 (AI Native Individual) → Level 4 (AI Native Team). Self-assessment: where is each person today?
- **Plan, Execute, Verify**: the engineer's new job. Coding is not the bottleneck — thinking clearly is.
- **The Boris Cherny case study**: 300 PRs/month, 10-15 parallel agents, plan-first discipline, auto-ship pipeline.
- **Beyond code**: AI for operations, meetings, planning, customer outreach, content creation. Everything the team does can be rethought.
- **What we will not do anymore**: websites as they are today may not be the future. Where is the market going? What does the agentic web mean for our work?

**Output**: Each person identifies their current AI maturity level and one concrete workflow to restructure this week.

### Afternoon Lab (13:30 – 16:00): Tool Setup & First Agent Runs
**Format**: Guided hands-on

Everyone sets up their AI-native workstation:
- Claude Code with 3+ terminal sessions
- Shared `CLAUDE.md` with team conventions checked into a repo
- First exercise: take a real task from the backlog, write a plan in plan mode, execute with an agent, review the output
- Pair up for troubleshooting and discovery

**Goal**: Everyone leaves Day 2 having completed at least one agent-driven task end-to-end. Those already comfortable push further: multi-agent parallel runs, auto-ship pipelines.

### Check-in (16:00 – 16:30)
What did you build? What surprised you? What broke?

### Session 3 (16:30 – 17:30): Guest — Cedric
**Format**: Talk + Q&A (last session of the day)

Cedric's perspective:
- The "2% vs 10% brain refactoring" concept: when you learn something new, how much of your existing knowledge refactors? Short iterations and continuously refactored plans.
- Why long-lasting plans break in the AI era
- How rapid experimentation changes the relationship between learning and doing
- What this means for our team's operating model

### Closing Sentence
Each person: **"The thing I thought was impossible that I now think is possible..."**

## Day 3 — BUILD: Hands-On, Day One

**Wednesday | Full day | Full hands-on with lightning talks**

**Theme**: Start building. Individuals or pairs, not whole-team projects. The goal: everybody does something they thought would not be possible the day before. Go fast — you're a crew of 1-2 people plus agents.

> From today, the format shifts to full hands-on. The daily check-in at 16:00 is the team's sync point. Lightning talks and internal knowledge sessions are woven into the build rhythm.

### Morning Spark (9:00 – 9:15)
**"We are an applied research team"**
Everything we build for ourselves is product research. Every workflow experiment is R&D. This week we're not doing exercises — we're shipping real things.

### Lightning Session (9:15 – 10:15): Know Our Products

Two internal deep-dives so everyone understands what the team has already built:

1. **Experience Catalyst** (30 min) — Karl / Natalia
   - What it does, the architecture, how it's deployed
   - Live: everyone grabs an instance and tries it
   - Discussion: what changes when we build this with agent fleets?

2. **Digital Insights** (30 min) — Jose
   - What it does, how it's used across sales stages
   - Where it's expanding (Commerce, Workfront)
   - Discussion: what customer intelligence becomes possible with AI-native approaches?

### Sales Perspective Session (10:30 – 11:30): Day in the Life of a Salesperson
**Format**: Guest talk + Q&A (Nicole organizing)

A sales practitioner shares:
- How they use AEM in the field today
- What helps close deals, what's missing
- What they wish the product could do
- How engineering can support the sales motion

> *Nicole to confirm speaker (Sebastian's team). If available, schedule here or adjacent to the product sessions.*

### Build Time (11:30 – 16:00, with lunch break)
**Format**: Individuals or pairs, self-directed

Choose what to build. The crazier the idea, the better — but it must be real, not a toy. Suggested starting points:

| Idea | Description | Type |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Overnight Experiment Factory** | A backlog of experiments that builds by itself and executes overnight. Agents generate ideas, implement approaches, critique each other, elect winners. You arrive in the morning to ranked prototypes. | Automation |
| **AI-Driven Team Collaboration Space** | A shared intelligence layer where there's no need for provisioning meetings, asking questions, or manual knowledge sharing. The system knows what everyone is doing — even from code. | Infrastructure |
| **Automated Demo Delivery** | POC code → summary → demo script → screen recording → voiceover → Slack post. Zero manual effort. Every experiment gets visibility by default. | Automation |
| **Build-and-Sell-It Challenge** | Build something end-to-end that could be sold. Make it serious: a startup you shut down the day after, but someone could buy it with a credit card. End-to-end, production-grade. | Moonshot |
| **Zero-Touch Meeting Pipeline** | Meeting ends → transcript → shared context → key learnings + next actions. No manual notes, no "meeting notes tax." | Automation |
| **Feedback-to-Fix Pipeline** | Slack feedback → classify → open branch → generate PR → notify reviewer. Customer voice to shipped code. | Automation |
| **Your own idea** | Personal ideas are welcome and encouraged. The only requirement: it should be something you thought would not be possible the day before. | Anything |

**Rules**:
- Work alone or in pairs — small is fast
- Use multi-agent workflows (minimum 3 parallel sessions if you can)
- Plan-first: iterate with the agent until the plan is solid, then execute
- Document what you build in a shareable format (the Cookbook)

### Check-in (16:00 – 16:30)
Quick sync: what's everyone working on, early learnings, any blockers. Not formal demos yet — save those for Friday.

### Closing Sentence
Each person: **"The thing I learned today that changes how I think about my work..."**

### Overnight Agents (Optional)
Leave agents running overnight on your project. Seed them with clear instructions. Review results Thursday morning.

**Friday | Full day | Final build + demos + commitments**

> **Paolo is out today.** Mike and Karl close the workshop.

## Design Principles Behind This Schedule

1. **Monday–Tuesday = discuss + inspire, Wednesday–Friday = build.** The week ramps from blended sessions to pure hands-on. By Wednesday, the team is self-directed.

2. **Half-day Monday.** Most people travel Monday morning. The workshop starts at noon and makes the most of the afternoon with mission alignment + Philippe's session.

3. **Hands-on > slides.** Three full days of building. The team learns by doing. The things they build are real — not exercises, not throwaway.

4. **Daily rhythm.** Same check-in time (16:00) every day. Same closing sentence ritual. Nicole's proposal: a predictable cadence that creates team cohesion even when people are deep in individual work.

5. **Know your own products.** Wednesday morning's Experience Catalyst + Digital Insights sessions ensure everyone understands what the team has built, before building the next thing.

6. **Designed for partial attendance.** Paolo is out Thu–Fri. Constantin and Natalia miss Monday. Tim misses Tue–Wed. Every session is recorded and summarized with AI. The build days are self-directed enough that missing one doesn't break the arc.

7. **Fun is the leading indicator.** "The crazier the idea, the better." The build-and-sell-it challenge. The overnight factory. The pirate mandate. If people aren't energized by Day 3, adjust.

8. **Real customer connection.** Philippe on Monday, sales perspective on Wednesday, customer-facing demos on Friday. The AI transformation isn't abstract — it's in service of the mission: accelerating AEM growth and adoption.
