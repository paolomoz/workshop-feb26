# Happy But Sad

## The Mood
- Paint Anthropic impressionistically, like Claude Monet, a big broad stroke at a time
- Almost everyone is vibrantly happy
- Same crackle of electricity that Amazon had in 1998
- Everyone knows something amazing is about to happen that will change society forever

## The Gravity
- Every person and team feels sweetly but sadly transcendent
- A group shepherding something of civilization-level importance into existence
- Excited but solemn — an elvish old-world-fading-away gravity

## The Warning
- They feel genuinely sorry for a lot of companies
- 2026 will break many companies, and most don't see it coming
- Anthropic is trying to warn everyone — like yelling about an offshore earthquake to villages that haven't seen a tidal wave in a century

## Key Quote
> "It's like yelling about an offshore earthquake to villages that haven't seen a tidal wave in a century."
