# Improv at Scale

## "Yes, And..."
- Described as "Yes, and..." style improvisational theater
- Every idea welcomed, examined, savored, judged by the Hive Mind
- All based on vibes — no central decision-making authority
- Just trying everything — when magic happens, they all realize it at once

## Accretive Development
- Engineers sculpting together with clay
- Campfires around in-flight products, swarming, changing shapes, trying variations and mashups
- Claude Cowork launched publicly 10 days after the first idea
- Forward progress via mashups and exploration — floodfill search
- Pure functional data structures emerging at the organizational level

## Superhuman Velocity
- Engineers 10x-100x as productive as those using Cursor and chat today
- Roughly 1000x as productive as Googlers in 2005
- Like a quivering mass implementing Multi-Armed Bandit on ideas

## The Team Sport
- Hive mind ejects anyone not acting like a happy worker bee
- Contribute ideas in the right ways — death of the ego
- It's improv — a team sport — doesn't work to come in guns blazing
- Real power in "Yes, and..." but most companies learned to say No

## Key Quote
> "They are just trying everything, and when magic happens, they all just kind of realize it at once."
