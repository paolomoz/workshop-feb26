# The Vibe Mind

## Organized Chaos
- Not run like any other company this size
- Every other company becomes "professional," compartmentalized, accountable — Anthropic hasn't bothered
- Production systems are serious with world-class SREs and scaling engineers
- But the tail that wags the dog is Claude — the Work Generator

## The Hive Mind
- Self-evidently in the middle (or beginning) of a Golden Age — churny and frothy
- Employees describe it as a hive mind run entirely on vibes
- Organizations reflect their leaders — clearly directed by leadership, intentional
- Some key graph nodes spread through the hive mind keeping it stable

## The Centrifuge
- Interfere with the hive mind and you'll gently be pushed to the edges
- The centrifuge spins you to the periphery, carried by a wave of vibes
- Feels fragile, may have scaling ceilings
- But they've kept it going
