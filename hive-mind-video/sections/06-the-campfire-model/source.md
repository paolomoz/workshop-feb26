# The Campfire Model

## Beyond Silos
- Anthropic and SageOx both build together around a campfire
- Insight from Thoughtworks unconference in Deer Valley, Utah — Martin Fowler's event
- Spec-Driven Development felt like waterfall or Intentional Programming v2

## Evolutionary Development
- Everyone sits around a campfire together and builds
- Center of the campfire is a living prototype
- No waterfall. No spec. A prototype that evolves via group sculpting into the final product
- Something that finally feels right — you know it when you find it

## 90-Day Cycles
- Anthropic does not produce an operating plan ahead more than 90 days
- That is their outermost planning cycle
- Vibing on the shortest cycles and fastest feedback loops for their size
- The result is something like improv

## Key Quote
> "There is no waterfall. There is no spec. There is a prototype that simply evolves, via group sculpting, into the final product."
