# How To End a Golden Age

## What Is a Golden Age
- A period of intense innovation, category creation, velocity, productivity — lasts several years
- Attracts all the greatest talent in the industry, very quickly
- Amazon: Golden Age going strong when Yegge left in 2005
- Google: lasted until April 2011
- Microsoft: early 2000s, C#/.NET, greatest minds, shaped the industry

## The Formula
- During Golden Ages: more work than people
- When they crash: more people than work
- Jacob Gabrielson at Amazon: "Everyone here is always slightly oversubscribed"

## How Google Died
- Eric Schmidt: "Let a Thousand Flowers Bloom" — generating luck
- Larry Page, April 2011: "More Wood Behind Fewer Arrows" — cut funded work 50%+
- Kept every engineer but killed the work — empire building, territoriality, Cookie Licking
- One person described Microsoft as being a molecule in a metal, elbows tightly locked

## Anthropic's Position
- Smack in the middle of a Golden Age
- Far more work than people on all fronts — surface of an expanding sphere
- Never a reason to fight — infinite work
- Everyone gets many chances to put their ideas in the sun

## Key Quote
> "During Golden Ages, there is more work than people. When they crash, there are more people than work."
