# Intro — The Spaceship

## The Signal
- Something is happening at Anthropic — a spaceship beginning to take off
- Getting in has odds comparable to making it into the NFL
- Everyone is the best of the best of the best, to a crazier degree than Google at its peak

## The Investigation
- Long, frank conversations with nearly 40 people over four months
- Cofounders, execs, whole teams, AI research, Engineering, GTM, Sales, Editorial, Product
- Anthropic is unusually impenetrable — employees have every incentive to stay quiet
- But Yegge got them to open up

## The Thesis
- By talking to enough of them, began to suspect the future of software development is the Hive Mind

## Key Quote
> "Everyone I've met from Anthropic is the best of the best of the best."
