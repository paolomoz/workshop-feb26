# The Small Version

## SageOx — A 3-Person Hive Mind
- Ajit Banerjee, Ryan Snodgrass, Milkana Brace — little startup in Kirkland
- Above a coffee shop bakery, alternating between coding and sleeping for weeks
- All level 7-8 on the Dev Evolution to AI chart
- Operating a hive mind with three people — same patterns as Anthropic

## Radical Transparency
- External contributor wasted time acting on 2-hour-old info — too slow
- Need full transparency at all times or you'll fall irretrievably behind
- Volume way up: "I AM GOING DOWN TO GET A DONUT NOW." "I AM ALSO DELETING THE DATABASE." "OK."
- Entire work stream public — incredibly valuable for forensics
- Record conversations, transcripts auto-uploaded and versioned

## The Death of the Ego
- Everyone sees mistakes, wrong turns, exactly how fast you work
- Nothing to hide — you have to be a happy bee
- Most developers would be really uncomfortable with that

## Build for Yourself
- Building for yourself is the only way to give your product a nonzero chance
- Settlers of Catan: Teuber built games for his own family for years
- Too many AI founders guessing what people want — wrong side of the Bitter Lesson

## Key Quote
> "It's the death of the ego. Everyone can see all your mistakes and wrong turns."
