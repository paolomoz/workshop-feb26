# Infographic Plan — The Anthropic Hive Mind (8 Sections)

**Style**: Knolling (all sections)
**Format**: 1920x1080 landscape
**Reference**: Perfectly organized objects at 90-degree angles on flat surfaces, photographed from directly overhead. Material-authentic, shadow-casting, obsessively aligned.

---

## 1. Intro — The Spaceship
- **Surface**: Matte black felt
- **Composition**: Categories — distinct groups separated by wider gaps
- **Objects**:
  - Top-left cluster: 40 small identical white business cards fanned in a precise 5x8 grid — each representing one conversation
  - Top-right: A single brass NFL badge next to a single brushed-steel Anthropic employee badge — same size, implying comparable odds
  - Center: A large polished chrome rocket model lying flat, nose pointing right, dominating the composition
  - Bottom-left: A row of 6 small gold stars ascending in size — "best of the best of the best"
  - Bottom-right: A clean white label tag reading "THE HIVE MIND" in small Helvetica caps
- **Accent color**: Safety orange on the rocket's engine nozzle
- **Labels**: "40 INTERVIEWS" / "NFL ODDS" / "GOLDEN AGE" — small, white, sans-serif

## 2. Happy But Sad
- **Surface**: Warm concrete gray
- **Composition**: Comparison — two parallel columns, left vs. right
- **Objects**:
  - Left column (Happy): A crackling sparkler (frozen mid-spark), a vintage Amazon '98 shipping box miniature, a bright copper lightning bolt, a small glass vial of gold glitter, a warm Edison bulb
  - Right column (Sad): A small porcelain elf figurine, an antique hourglass with sand nearly gone, a single dried pressed flower, a tiny model village with houses, a dark glass wave paperweight
  - Center gap: A single label reading "SWEETLY BUT SADLY TRANSCENDENT"
  - Bottom: A small folded note card reading "2026" next to a miniature tsunami wave model approaching the tiny village
- **Accent color**: Cherry red on the lightning bolt
- **Labels**: "ELECTRICITY" / "GRAVITY" / "TIDAL WAVE"

## 3. The Vibe Mind
- **Surface**: Dark charcoal felt
- **Composition**: Networks — central object surrounded by radiating satellites

  - Corner: A small brass label plate reading "WORK GENERATOR"
- **Accent color**: Electric blue glow on the Claude chip
- **Labels**: "VIBES" / "CENTRIFUGE" / "HIVE MIND"
